# Movie taste — research brief

**Category:** Entertainment / film preference  
**Stage:** Persona-evaluation survey (not a box-office or marketing test)

## What this survey is about

We want to understand how different people choose movies for a typical night in. The questions mix
familiar titles with statements about crying, laughing, runtime, and prestige versus light entertainment.

## Titles you will be asked about

- **The Shawshank Redemption** — widely beloved prestige prison drama
- **Manchester by the Sea** — a heavy, grief-centered character drama
- **Bridesmaids** — an R-rated ensemble comedy
- **Superbad** — a coming-of-age teen comedy
- **Everything Everywhere All at Once** — a genre-bending film that is funny, chaotic, and emotional
- A **heavy art-house drama** (slow, serious, often subtitled or formally demanding)
- A **rom-com** (a light romantic comedy)

You do not need to have seen every title. Answer from your taste: what you would actually choose,
sit through, or avoid.

## What this survey is probing

- Preference for emotional / prestige drama versus comedy and lighter fare
- Willingness to sit through a long character drama
- Reactions to specific films and genres
- What you would watch tonight, and why
