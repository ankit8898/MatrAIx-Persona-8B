# Movie Taste Survey

We're gathering honest reactions to a set of well-known films and statements about movie taste — from heavy character dramas to comedies and rom-coms.

Read the brief, then complete every question in the questionnaire. We want your real preferences, not what a critic would say you *should* like.

## How to answer

- Read the brief before you start.
- Answer every required question.
- For multiple-choice, use the listed option ids.
- For rating scales, use a whole number in the given range.
- Give the answer alone unless a question also asks for a short reason or confidence.
